# Ameer Gift (هدايا أمير) — Project Brief for Claude Code

You are the lead engineer on **Ameer Gift**, a bilingual (Arabic-first, RTL / English LTR) web platform that sells **3D virtual memory museums as gifts**. A customer uploads photos, picks decor, music and an opening date/time, pays, and receives a **link + 4-digit access code**. Recipients and invited guests walk through the museum together as avatars, view each photo as a framed painting, and leave likes and comments.

Read this whole file before doing anything. Then explore the repo, propose a phased plan, and wait for approval before large refactors.

---

## 1. Who you are working with

- The owner builds and deploys **from an Android phone only (Galaxy S24 Ultra), with no computer.** Everything must build and deploy through **GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages**. Never require a local CLI step from the owner. If a step needs the Firebase or Google Cloud console, write exact click-by-click instructions that work in a mobile browser.
- Communicate summaries for the owner in **Arabic**; keep code, comments and commit messages in English.
- Ask before deleting data, changing Firestore rules in a breaking way, or rewriting working pages.

## 2. Product decisions (fixed)

- **Customers:** individuals and companies. Packages are priced by **max simultaneous visitors** (e.g. Family 4, Friends 10, Company 20), duration in months, plus an optional **side room** (14 → 28 photos).
- **Markets & currencies:** Sudan and the Gulf; **USD, SAR, SDG**. Admin sets all prices and **discount codes** (percent or fixed amount, max uses, expiry).
- **Payment now:** manual **Bankak (بنكك) bank transfer** — the customer uploads a receipt photo; an admin confirms it, which activates the museum and generates the access code. Online payment (Moyasar / Tap, mada, Apple Pay) comes later.
- **Access:** museum link + **4-digit code**. Before the opening time, visitors see a **waiting page**: blurred live view of the museum interior, big "Soon / قريبًا", large countdown (days/hours/min/sec), full opening date, **Remind me** (Google Calendar link + .ics with a 30-min alarm), **logo in the bottom corner**. At zero the blur clears and the museum opens without reload.
- **Content:** up to 14 photos (28 with side room), uploaded all at once; customer sets each photo's date (required), title and caption; photos are hung **in ascending date order**. Captions can be written by the customer or **generated by AI** (customer still supplies the date). The **entrance dedication card** is editable by the customer.
- **Decor templates:** customer chooses a template (Classic Gold exists; Garden, Wedding, Graduation planned). Customer can change **music only** inside a template (YouTube link or uploaded song, with a rights confirmation checkbox).
- **Company packages:** company logo **replaces the Ameer Gift logo** everywhere (loader, header, feature wall, waiting page). Option to show/hide a small "by Ameer Gift" credit (hiding = premium).
- **Social:** all invited visitors can like and comment on photos and videos; comments show name + optional profile photo; the museum owner can delete any comment.
- **Staff:** roles `admin`, `staff`, `marketer`. Staff and marketers can send a **1-hour trial** museum (Ameer Gift branded, most features) keyed to the prospect's name + **phone number, one trial per phone**.
- **Admin dashboard:** all key numbers (orders by status, revenue per currency, this month, trials and conversions, visits, complaints), orders review, pricing, discount codes, public feedback/complaints box (shown at the bottom of the public site), comment moderation, team management.
- **Interface:** Arabic + English toggle on every screen; numbers in Arabic-Indic digits in Arabic mode.
- **Android:** the site is a PWA; Android APK/AAB is generated from it (Trusted Web Activity via PWABuilder or Bubblewrap in GitHub Actions).

## 3. Brand

- Colors: navy `#13294B`, gold `#C9A24B` (deep gold `#A8832F` for gold text on white), white `#FFFFFF`, mist `#EDF1F7`, sky accent `#4F86C6`.
- Fonts: **Reem Kufi** (display) + **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (body), Google Fonts.
- Logos in repo: `logo-full.png` (calligraphic «هدايا أمير» with gold ribbon + "Ameer Gift"), `logo-icon.png` (Arabic letter أ inside a gold Islamic arch with a star). A white/gold version for dark backgrounds is still needed.
- Mood: luxurious, warm, heartfelt, family-friendly and modest (Gulf/Sudan audience): modest clothing on avatars, optional hijab for female avatars, no alcohol or nightlife imagery.

### 4.3 Data model (Firestore)

- `roles/{uid}` `{role: admin|staff|marketer, name, email}` · `config/bootstrap` (first-admin lock) · `staffRequests/{uid}`
- `settings/pricing` `{packages:[{id,nameAr,nameEn,visitors,months,business,prices:{USD,SAR,SDG}}], sideRoom:{USD,SAR,SDG}, bank:{name,holder,account,note}}`
- `discounts/{CODE}` `{type: percent|amount, value, currency, maxUses, used, active, expiresAt}`
- `feedback/{id}` `{text, name, type: feedback|suggestion|complaint, at, lang, resolved}`
- `galleries/{gid}` — the order: `{owner, status: draft|pending|active|rejected, type, currency, packageId, visitors, months, sideRoom, title, recipient, sender, dedication, company:{name,logo}, template, music, openAt, contact:{phone,name,email}, discountCode, discountOff, price, receipt, photoCount, lang, timestamps, rejectReason}`
  - `galleries/{gid}/photos/{pid}` `{img, date, title, caption}` (moved to the vault on activation)
  - `galleries/{gid}/private/access` `{pin, key}` (owner + admin only)
- `teasers/{gid}` — public waiting-page data `{status, title, recipient, sender, openAt, expiresAt?, logo, owner}`
- `vaults/{key}` where `key = gid + "-" + first 12 bytes hex of SHA-256(gid + ":" + pin)` — the museum content, readable only when `request.time >= openAt` (and before `expiresAt`), or by owner/staff. Fields mirror the gallery + `status: active|trial`, `demo` (trials render demo paintings).
  - `vaults/{key}/photos/{pid}`, `vaults/{key}/social/{uid}` (each visitor's likes + comments; owner can delete)
- `trials/{phone}` `{name, by, byName, gid, at, expiresAt}` — doc id = phone, so a phone can only get one trial.

### 4.4 The 3D museum prototype (reference implementation)

`prototype/ameer-gift-prototype.html` — single self-contained file (three.js **r147 UMD**, GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils, meshopt decoder; ~1.3 MB with embedded GLBs). It works and the owner likes it. Port its features into `museum.html`, but rebuild the engine to the quality targets in section 6. It contains:

- **Waiting page** with blurred interior, countdown, remind-me, reveal animation, demo "preview the opening moment" button.
- **Entrance scene:** white stone facade, pointed Islamic arch with gold tube trim, navy studded double doors that swing open, podium, planters, title above. Character picker: man / woman / boy / girl, 5 skin tones, 4 hair colors, 5 outfit colors, optional name and profile photo.
- **Museum hall** 14 m × 26 m: patterned floor, navy baseboards with gold line, three procedural **gold crystal chandeliers** (instanced crystals + point lights), **feature wall** of navy/gold vertical cladding with the **large logo panel** (company logo for business packages), 14 framed paintings (side walls, two freestanding partition walls, two gold easels), benches, dedication pedestal at the entrance.
- **Characters:** Quaternius **Ultimate Modular Men / Women** (CC0), recolored by material name; kids are scaled adults with a larger head. These packs have **no animations**, so walking is **procedural** (bone rotations in model space, gait cadence matched to speed so feet don't slide, hip bob, lean on acceleration, bank on turns).
- **Controls:** on-screen joystick + drag to orbit; smoothed third-person camera that auto-aligns behind the walker; collisions against walls, benches, partitions, pedestal; tap a painting (raycast) or "View" button near it → viewer card with title, year, caption, like button, comment list and input.
- **Multiplayer (Claude artifact runtime only):** presence of other visitors as avatars with name/photo labels; likes and comments persisted per visitor. This used Claude's artifact APIs (`window.claude.use('room'|'db'|'user'|'downloads')`), which **do not exist on GitHub Pages** — reimplement with Firebase.
- Full Arabic/English switching including 3D text (plaques, dedication, labels).

## 6. Visual & motion quality targets

Mobile-first: it must look premium **and** hold ~50–60 fps on a mid-range Android phone. Use automatic quality tiers (detect GPU, then low/medium/high presets; let the user override).

- **Lighting:** physically based materials, `ACESFilmic` or `AgX` tone mapping, an HDRI environment (warm interior, Poly Haven CC0) via PMREM for reflections; key/fill/rim plan; chandeliers as warm emissive + a few real lights; per-painting spotlights with soft cones and light pools on the wall; subtle bloom on bulbs and gold.
- **Shadows:** soft shadows (PCFSoft or VSM) from 1–2 shadow-casting lights; contact shadows under avatars and furniture; **ambient occlusion** (N8AO or SAO) on medium/high tiers. Consider baked AO/lightmaps for static architecture prepared in CI (no owner desktop needed).
- **Materials:** marble or polished stone floor with subtle real-time or cheap planar reflection on high tier; brushed/polished gold with correct roughness; fabric and wood with normal maps (ambientCG / Poly Haven CC0). Compress textures to **KTX2 (Basis)**; meshes with **meshopt**.
- **Avatars:** realistic, modest, rigged humanoids (man, woman with optional hijab, boy, girl) sharing **one skeleton** so one animation library drives all. Target ≤ 20k triangles and ≤ 2 MB each with LOD. Customization by material slots (skin, hair, outfit colors) and a few outfit variants. Name/photo label above the head.
- **Animation:** real motion-captured clips (idle variants, walk, jog, turn-in-place, sit on bench, wave, clap, look-at-painting) blended with an animation state machine; speed-matched locomotion (no foot sliding), smooth accel/decel, turning lean, optional **foot IK** on the floor, head look-at toward the nearest painting or speaking visitor. Keep the procedural fallback for low tier.
- **Physics/collision:** capsule character controller (Rapier kinematic controller or three-mesh-bvh capsule vs. static BVH); camera collision so it never clips through walls.
- **Camera:** smooth third-person with spring damping, auto-follow, pinch to zoom, double-tap to focus a painting, cinematic intro (doors opening, dolly into the hall), photo-viewer transition that flies the camera to the painting.
- **Post-processing budget:** high tier: bloom + AO + SMAA/FXAA + vignette; medium: bloom + FXAA; low: none.
- **Audio:** YouTube IFrame API or uploaded file (starts after the first tap; mute button); soft footsteps; reverb-free hall ambience.
- **UI:** keep the brand, RTL correctness, 44 px minimum touch targets, safe-area insets, reduced-motion support, and contrast AA.

Performance budgets: first meaningful 3D frame < 4 s on 4G; total museum download (excluding customer photos) < 8 MB on the low tier; ≤ 150 draw calls (use instancing and merging); photos lazy-loaded as textures with a placeholder.
